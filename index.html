What this is not

  • Three cycles is three data points. Two differences between the bull multipliers, one ratio between those differences. Nothing here can be tested for significance; a pattern that has repeated three times is a pattern that has not failed yet.
  • One prediction landing is one prediction landing. It was a good hit — the table above says how good — but a single confirmed forecast is the weakest evidence there is, because the forecasts that missed are not collected anywhere.
  • A number for 2100 is an assumption, not a finding. The three continuations above start from identical history and end orders of magnitude apart. Anyone quoting one of them as the projection is quoting their own assumption.
  • The bottom the pattern points at has not been confirmed here — — and its depth is a guess in every scenario on this page.
  • Pattern extrapolation, published for interest. Not investment advice.
